# Miro Collaboration

## Purpose
Describe a consistent Miro board layout for story mapping so stakeholders, BAs, and the delivery team can collaborate on scope and sequencing in one shared visual space.

## When to use
When a feature or release needs stakeholder alignment on scope and priority before or alongside story writing — typically during context analysis and story splitting.

## How
1. Create a board with a top row of actor/journey-step cards (the "backbone") representing the user's end-to-end workflow, left to right in chronological order.
2. Under each backbone step, add a column of story cards (the "walking skeleton" row first — minimum viable path — then additional rows below for later releases).
3. Color-code cards by status (e.g., draft, ready, in progress, done) and by persona owner (BA, Dev, QA, UX) using a documented legend on the board.
4. Add a "parking lot" area for out-of-scope ideas and a "questions" area for open items surfaced during the session, so they are captured without derailing the map.
5. After the session, transcribe the backbone and prioritized rows back into the story backlog; the board is a working artifact, not the system of record.

## Guardrails
- Specifications/context are the source of truth.
- Never output secrets or board access links/credentials in generated content.
